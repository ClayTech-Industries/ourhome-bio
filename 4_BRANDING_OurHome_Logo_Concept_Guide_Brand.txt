# OurHome — Logo Concept Guide

**Brand:** OurHome — AI companion home app  
**Tagline:** "It holds space."  
**Palette:** Golden Amber `#D4A853` · Deep Charcoal `#1A1A1A` · Warm Cream `#F5F0E8`

---

## Concept 1 — Brushstroke Logomark + Wordmark *(Light)*
A single fluid golden amber brushstroke traces both the roofline and doorway of a home in one expressive gesture. Organic, calligraphic, not geometric. The 'OurHome' wordmark sits below in a refined humanist sans-serif. Feels: Parisian boutique meets warm welcome home.
**Best for:** Website hero, pitch deck cover, print

---

## Concept 2 — Glowing Archway Logomark + Wordmark *(Dark)*
Layered amber arches with a luminous warm glow emanating from the center — like light from inside your home. 'OurHome' in cream on charcoal. Feels: Intimate, magical, Ghibli-esque. The standout concept.
**Best for:** App splash screen, dark mode, marketing materials

---

## Concept 3 — Wordmark Only *(Light + Tagline)*
'OurHome' in a sleek geometric sans-serif — the 'O' glowing with warm amber light from within. All letters clean and distinct. 'It holds space.' in whisper-thin weight below. Feels: Modern, minimal, quietly warm.
**Best for:** App store listing, small sizes, email signature

---

## Concept 4 — Minimal Symbol *(App Icon, Dark)*
Two soft organic shapes lean into each other, their meeting point forming a rooftop peak and open doorway. Abstract but immediately readable. Golden amber + cream on charcoal. Glows at center. Feels: Together, home, memory — without a word.
**Best for:** App icon, favicon, sticker, icon-only contexts

---

## Concept 5 — Warm Emotional Variant *(Painterly, Light)*
A watercolor-style glowing doorway arch in golden amber — two soft silhouettes in the threshold. 'OurHome' in a humanist letterform below. Tagline 'It holds space.' whispered beneath. Feels: A memory you love. Less startup, more Studio Ghibli.
**Best for:** Onboarding screen, emotional brand moments, social media

---

## Concept 6 — Together Symbol + Wordmark *(Dark)*
Two teardrop silhouettes lean toward each other, their negative space forming the doorway and roofline of a home. 'OurHome' in cream below. 'It holds space.' in amber. Conceptually rich: two beings, one home. Feels: Intimate, human, sophisticated.
**Best for:** Dark mode, pitch deck, merchandise

---

## Usage Notes
- All logos are **4K resolution, 1:1 square**
- Concepts 1, 3, 5 → **light backgrounds** (warm cream)
- Concepts 2, 4, 6 → **dark backgrounds** (deep charcoal)
- Concept 4 works as a standalone **app icon** at any size
- Concepts 2 and 6 are the **strongest** for a primary brand identity
