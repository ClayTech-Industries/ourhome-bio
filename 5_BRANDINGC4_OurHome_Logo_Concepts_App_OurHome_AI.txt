# OurHome — Logo Concepts

**App:** OurHome — AI companion home app  
**Tagline:** "It holds space."  
**Brand Colors:** Golden Amber `#D4A853` · Deep Charcoal `#1A1A1A` · Warm Cream `#F5F0E8`

---

## Concept 1 — Logomark + Wordmark
Two overlapping arch shapes forming a home silhouette with golden amber light radiating from the center. Paired with the "OurHome" wordmark and tagline.
- **Dark version** (charcoal bg): Best for splash screens, pitch decks, dark UI
- **Light version** (cream bg): Best for marketing materials, onboarding, web

## Concept 2 — Wordmark Only
Pure typographic treatment — "OurHome" in a modern elegant sans-serif. The capital **O** glows warm amber inside; the capital **H** is shaped as a subtle doorframe/arch.
- **Dark version**: Sophisticated, premium, pitch-ready
- **Light version**: Clean, airy, editorial — great for press kits

## Concept 3 — Minimal App Icon
Standalone abstract glyph: home archway + interlocking forms suggesting togetherness. Warm amber glow at core. Works at 48px and scales beautifully. No text.

## Concept 4 — Warm / Emotional Variant
Painterly golden light spilling from a house silhouette — Studio Ghibli meets luxury brand. Serif wordmark "OurHome" with the tagline "It holds space." in delicate italic gold. Most emotional and human of the set.

---

## Usage Guidance
| Context | Recommended |
|---|---|
| App icon (iOS/Android) | Concept 3 |
| Splash screen | Concept 1 Dark |
| Pitch deck hero | Concept 4 or Concept 1 Light |
| Website header | Concept 2 Light |
| Dark UI / dark mode | Concept 1 Dark or Concept 2 Dark |
| Social / OG images | Concept 4 |
